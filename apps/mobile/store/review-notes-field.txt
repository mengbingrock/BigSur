Labee is a research notebook and assistant, used mainly for bench science: a researcher keeps their protocols — experimental procedures such as a PCR setup, a plasmid miniprep or a Gibson assembly — as documents, organised by category and searchable by meaning. They can then chat with an assistant that has those protocols in front of it and get answers grounded in them. The assistant is general-purpose and built on Claude's agent tooling, so it can also answer ordinary technical questions and will sometimes mention its own tools or optional third-party integrations.

The iPhone app is a companion to the Labee desktop app (macOS). The desktop app holds the protocols and runs the assistant on the user's own computer; the phone reads those protocols and continues the conversations away from the computer. The phone can also talk to our server directly, without a Mac, which is how the attached video was recorded.

SETUP AND ACCESS
1. Open the app. Leave the Server field at its default (https://labee.online). Sign in with the demo account email and password supplied above.
2. That account connects to its linked Mac automatically. The Sessions tab lists three existing conversations; the Protocols tab lists twelve bench protocols. (The Runs tab is for long-running multi-step analyses and is empty.)
3. Open any conversation and send a message — for example "Summarise the Gibson assembly protocol in three steps". Replies take about 20-40 seconds.
4. Tap New to start a fresh conversation. Open any protocol to read it; the Protocols tab can also search them or answer a question from them.
5. Accounts are created on our website (labee.online) or by "Sign in with Google", which creates an account on first use; the app has no separate registration form. Account deletion is in the app: Settings > Delete account, as the video shows. Please do not delete the demo account itself.

No sample files are needed; the demo account is pre-populated.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- labee.online — our own hosted service (AWS). Accounts, sign-in, and the relay that connects the phone to the user's Mac.
- Google Sign-In — optional alternative to email sign-in.
- Anthropic Claude and OpenAI GPT — the models the assistant is built on. Inference runs on the user's own Mac using their own account with those providers, or through our service on a metered plan.
- Stripe — payment processing for plans bought on our website. There are no in-app purchases; nothing is sold inside the iOS app.
- Expo (EAS) — build tooling and push notifications.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions. The interface is English only at this time.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Labee is not in a regulated industry and handles no medical, financial or health data. The twelve starter protocols are original, written by us for general molecular-biology teaching use, not reproduced from any publisher. All other content is created by the user.
