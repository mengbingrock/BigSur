## Medium

For 400 ml of LB: 4 g NaCl, 4 g tryptone, 2 g yeast extract, water to 400 ml in a
500 ml bottle. Cap loosely with foil, autoclave, cool to room temperature, then
seal.

## Antibiotics (working concentration)

| Antibiotic | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| Ampicillin | 100 ug/ml |
| Kanamycin | 50 ug/ml |
| Tetracycline | 10 ug/ml |
| Chloramphenicol | 25 ug/ml |

Stocks are made at 1000X, so dilute 1:1000 — 100 ul of a 100 mg/ml stock into
100 ml of LB.

## Procedure

1. Pour LB into a tube or flask and add the antibiotic.
2. Touch a sterile tip or toothpick to a single colony.
3. Drop it into the medium and swirl.
4. Cover loosely with foil, or use a vented cap. The culture needs air.
5. Grow at 37 C for 12-18 h, shaking at 150-250 rpm.
6. Check it is cloudy before using it.

## Notes

- Some strains and plasmids want 30 C, which takes longer.
- High-copy plasmids are ready at 12-16 h; low-copy ones may need 18-30 h.
- Run a tube of LB plus antibiotic with no bacteria as a contamination control.
