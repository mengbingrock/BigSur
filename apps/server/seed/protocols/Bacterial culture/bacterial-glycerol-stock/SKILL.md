## Procedure

1. Grow an overnight liquid culture.
2. In a 2 ml screw-top tube or cryovial, mix 500 ul of the overnight culture with
   500 ul of 50% glycerol. Invert gently 5-6 times until uniform. The final
   glycerol concentration is 25%.
3. Freeze at -80 C.

## Recovering the strain

Scrape frozen material from the surface with a sterile loop, toothpick or tip
without thawing the stock, and streak it onto an LB agar plate. Incubate
overnight at whatever temperature the plasmid calls for.

## Notes

- Make the 50% glycerol by diluting 100% glycerol in water, then autoclave it.
- Use screw-top tubes. Snap-tops can spring open at -80 C.
- Minimise freeze-thaw cycles; never leave the stock out to thaw.
- Label the tube and the lid. Frost hides side labels.
