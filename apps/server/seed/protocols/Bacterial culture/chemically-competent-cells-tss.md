## Materials

TSS buffer chilled at 4 C, LB, ice, 50 ml falcon tubes, and one chilled
Eppendorf per millilitre of culture.

## Procedure

1. Grow a 5 ml seed culture to saturation, then dilute it 1/100 into 25-50 ml of
   fresh LB in a 200 ml conical flask.
2. Incubate at 30-37 C, 250-400 rpm, until OD600 reaches 0.2-0.5. Do not overshoot;
   competence falls away as the culture leaves exponential growth.
3. Chill the Eppendorfs on ice and pre-chill the TSS.
4. Split the culture into two 50 ml falcon tubes and stand them on ice for 10 min.
5. Spin 10 min at 3000 rpm, 4 C.
6. Pour off the supernatant and pipette out what is left.
7. Resuspend in TSS at 10% of the original culture volume, or 3-5% for more
   concentrated cells. Vortex gently.
8. Aliquot 100 ul into the chilled tubes.
9. Freeze at -80 C. A dry-ice bath or liquid nitrogen freezes them faster, which
   helps.

## Notes

Everything after step 4 happens at 4 C, and the cells stay on ice throughout.
Unused cells survive one refreeze with some loss of competence.
