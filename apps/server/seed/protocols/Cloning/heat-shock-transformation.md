  Transform chemically competent E. coli: 30-60 s at 42 C, 45 min outgrowth,
  plate on selection.

## Materials

- Chemically competent cells, kept at -80 C until use
- Plasmid DNA, 10 pg to 100 ng
- LB agar plates with the appropriate antibiotic
- LB or SOC without antibiotic
- 42 C water bath, ice, 37 C incubator

## Procedure

1. Thaw the competent cells on ice, 20-30 min.
2. Bring the plates to room temperature, or warm them at 37 C.
3. Add 1-5 ul of DNA to 20-50 ul of cells. Mix by flicking the tube; do not pipette.
4. Leave on ice 20-30 min.
5. Heat shock: put the lower half to two thirds of the tube in the 42 C bath for
   30-60 s. 45 s is the usual choice.
6. Return to ice for 2 min.
7. Add 250-1000 ul LB or SOC with no antibiotic and shake at 37 C for 45 min.
   This outgrowth is what lets the cells make the resistance protein before they
   meet the drug; skipping it loses most of your transformants.
8. Plate 50 ul on one plate and the remainder on a second.
9. Incubate overnight at 37 C.

## Notes

- A ligation transforms roughly ten-fold worse than an intact plasmid. Expect it.
- Plasmids over about 10 kb do better by electroporation.
