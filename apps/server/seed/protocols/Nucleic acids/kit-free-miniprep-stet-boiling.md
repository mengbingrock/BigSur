  Cheap high-throughput plasmid prep with lysozyme and a 40 s boil; clean enough
  to sequence.

## Buffers

**STET:** 8% sucrose, 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 8, 0.5% Triton X-100, 50 mM EDTA
**TE:** 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8, 1 mM EDTA

## Procedure

1. Transfer 1.5 ml of overnight culture to a tube and spin 5 min at 5000 rpm.
2. Pour off the supernatant.
3. Add 300 ul STET and vortex to resuspend fully.
4. Add 10 ul lysozyme at 10 mg/ml, vortex, and hold the tube in boiling water for
   40 s. Time this: longer shears the plasmid.
5. Spin 30 min at maximum speed, 4 C.
6. Lift the gelatinous debris pellet out with a toothpick and discard it.
7. Add 300 ul ice-cold isopropanol, or a 2:1 isopropanol:ammonium acetate mix.
8. Spin 10 min at maximum speed, 4 C.
9. Remove the supernatant.
10. Add 200 ul ice-cold 80% ethanol and spin 5 min at maximum speed, 4 C.
11. Remove the supernatant.
12. Air dry at room temperature or 37 C, or use a speedvac.
13. Rehydrate in 50 ul TE.

## Notes

This is the cheap route when you need many plasmids at once. The DNA is clean
enough for sequencing and for use as a PCR template.
