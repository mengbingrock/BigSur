## Choosing the percentage

| Agarose | Resolves |
| --- | --- |
| 0.5% | 1-30 kb |
| 0.7% | 0.8-12 kb |
| 1.0% | 0.5-10 kb |
| 1.2% | 0.4-7 kb |
| 1.5% | 0.2-3 kb |

Buffer is TAE, TBE or sodium borate. Sodium borate takes 200-250 V without the
gel melting, which is useful when you are in a hurry.

## Where the dyes run

Xylene cyanol runs with 10,000-5,000 bp on a 0.5-1.5% gel, and with about 750 bp
on a 2-3% gel. Bromophenol blue runs with 400-500 bp, and about 100 bp on a
2-3% gel. Pick a dye that will not hide your band.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the agarose into a beaker with buffer. About 50 ml suits a 7 x 10 cm tray.
2. Microwave at full power for roughly 90 s, or half power for 3 min, until no
   lens-shaped grains remain.
3. Cool about 5 min, until you can hold the beaker, then add the stain.
4. Tape the ends of the tray.
5. Pour, knock out any bubbles, seat the comb, and leave to set for 30 min.
6. Put the gel in the box and cover with running buffer.
7. Load the samples.
8. Run, then image.

## Notes

If an ethidium bromide gel looks purple, the stain is at least ten times too
concentrated.
