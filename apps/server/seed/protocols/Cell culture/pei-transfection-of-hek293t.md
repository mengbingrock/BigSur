---
name: PEI transfection of HEK293T
description: >-
  Transfect a 10 cm plate with 18.9 ug DNA and PEI at 1:3, with a chloroquine
  pre-treatment.
kind: protocol
category: Cell culture
---
## Materials

DMEM high glucose with 10% FBS and 4 mM L-alanyl-L-glutamine; Opti-Pro SFM;
25 mM chloroquine diphosphate; linear PEI, MW 25,000, at 1 mg/ml; 10 cm plates.

## Per 10 cm plate

| Component | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| DNA | 18.9 ug in 500 ul Opti-Pro SFM |
| PEI, 1 mg/ml | 56.7 ul in 443.3 ul Opti-Pro SFM (1:3 DNA:PEI) |
| Chloroquine | 10 ul of 25 mM per 10 ml medium |

## Procedure

1. **Day 0.** Seed HEK293T at 3.8 x 10^6 cells per 10 cm plate in complete DMEM.
   Incubate about 20 h at 37 C, 5% CO2.
2. **Day 1, five hours before transfecting.** Replace the medium with complete
   DMEM containing chloroquine.
3. **Day 1.** Dilute the DNA and the PEI separately in Opti-Pro SFM, combine
   them, and leave 15-20 min. Add dropwise to the cells.
4. Incubate 18 h.
5. **Day 2, morning.** Replace with fresh complete DMEM.
6. **Day 3 onward.** Look for expression after a further 24-48 h.

## Notes

- Titrate DNA:PEI from 1:1 to 1:6 for each new batch of PEI; batches vary.
- Use endotoxin-free plasmid DNA.
- Keep the cells below passage 20.
- No antibiotics in the medium during transfection.
