Privacy Policy

Labee, operated by TrueGrit AI, Inc.

Last updated 21 September 2026

Labee connects your phone or tablet to coding agent sessions running on your own Mac. This page explains exactly what we collect, which companies receive it, and how long it is kept. We have tried to describe what the software actually does rather than reserve every possible right.

The short version. We collect your email address and the content of your agent conversations. We do not run analytics, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising. Conversation content is sent to the AI provider that answers it, and voice recordings are sent to a speech-to-text provider and are not stored by us.

Who we are

Labee is operated by TrueGrit AI, Inc. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, write to support@labee.online.

What we collect

Account

  • Email address. This is the only identifier tied to your account.
  • A password hash, if you sign up with a password. We never store the password itself.
  • A Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google. Google returns your name and profile picture during sign-in; Labee discards both and stores neither.

Conversations and agent activity

When you use an agent, the following is stored so that a session survives being closed and can be picked up on another device:

  • The messages you send and the replies you receive, in full.
  • The agent's tool activity, including commands it ran and file contents it read. If you point an agent at a folder, the contents of files it opens there are part of the conversation record.
  • The working folder path for the session. Note that a path such as /Users/yourname/project usually contains your computer account name.
  • A session title, which is normally derived from your first message.

Where this is stored depends on how you run Labee. When the agent runs on your own Mac, the record lives on that Mac. If you connect your phone or tablet, a copy of recent sessions, their events and their most recent messages is mirrored to our server so that your devices can still read them when your Mac is asleep or offline.

Voice

When you hold the microphone button, the app records audio and sends it for transcription. Our server holds the recording in memory only, passes it to the speech-to-text provider, and returns the text. We do not write voice recordings to disk and we do not store them. On your device, the recording is written to the app's private storage as a temporary file, which iOS reclaims; the app does not upload it anywhere else.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a push token for your device. Note that a notification itself carries the session title and up to 140 characters of the agent's reply, so that content passes through Expo's push service and Apple's notification service on its way to your lock screen.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, which acts as merchant of record. Card numbers and billing details are entered on Stripe's own pages and never reach Labee's servers. We send Stripe your email address so your purchase can be matched to your account, and we store your plan, credit balance and usage totals.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because we do not track you.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or attribution software of any kind.
  • No contacts, photos, location, health data or calendar access.
  • No device fingerprint. The app tells the server only whether it is an iPhone, an iPad, an Android device or a browser.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records until you delete them. There is currently no automatic expiry, so a session you created a year ago is still there unless you removed it.

  • You can delete any individual session from the app, which removes its messages and events.
  • To delete your account and everything associated with it, write to support@labee.online and we will erase it, including any mirrored copies, within 30 days.

Security

Traffic between your devices and our server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Your session credential is held in the iOS keychain. If you supply your own AI provider API keys, they are encrypted before being stored. Conversation content itself is stored unencrypted at rest on the machine that holds it, which for most users is their own Mac.

Children

Labee is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Write to support@labee.online and we will respond. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
