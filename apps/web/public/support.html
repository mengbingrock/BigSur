Support

Help with Labee for iPhone, iPad and the Mac app.

Need a person? Write to support@labee.online. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. We usually reply within two business days.

Getting started

Labee has two halves. Your coding agent runs on your Mac, and the iPhone and iPad app attaches to those sessions so you can follow them and reply while you are away from your desk.

  1. Install the Labee desktop app on your Mac and sign in.
  2. In the desktop app, open Settings and connect it to your Labee account.
  3. On your iPhone or iPad, open Labee, sign in with the same account, and your Mac appears under your devices.
  4. Open any session to read it, or start a new one.

Common problems

My sessions do not appear on my phone

The phone can only see sessions from a Mac that has been connected to your account. Open the desktop app, go to Settings, and check that it reports a live connection. If it does not, connect it again. Sessions created before you connected will appear once the Mac syncs.

The microphone button does nothing

Check that Labee has microphone permission in the iOS Settings app, under Labee. Very short taps are ignored on purpose, so hold the button while you speak and release when you are done.

Voice input returns an error

Transcription happens on the server. If it fails repeatedly, it is usually a connectivity problem rather than a problem with your device. Try again on a different network, and write to us if it persists.

I am not getting notifications

Allow notifications for Labee in the iOS Settings app. Notifications are sent when a turn finishes or when the agent is waiting on your answer, so a session that is idle will not produce any.

Everything says the Mac is offline

Your Mac has to be awake and connected for live sessions. When it is asleep you can still read recent sessions, but you cannot send a new turn until it comes back.

Billing

Subscriptions and credit top-ups are handled by Stripe. You can change or cancel a plan from the billing section of the app, which opens Stripe's billing portal. For a refund or a billing question, write to support@labee.online.

Your data and your account

You can delete any individual session from the app, which removes its messages and its record of the agent's activity.

To delete your account and everything stored with it, write to support@labee.online from the address you signed up with. We will erase your account, your conversations and any mirrored copies within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

The privacy policy explains what is collected and who receives it.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running a current version of iOS or iPadOS.
  • A Mac running the Labee desktop app, for sessions that execute on your machine.
  • A Labee account.
